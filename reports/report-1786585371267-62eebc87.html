问题: 分析电子产品类目2024年的月度销售趋势并生成图表 识别意图: analyze

SQL 查询


SELECT DATE_FORMAT(created_at, '%Y-%m') AS sales_month, SUM(amount) AS total_sales_amount, COUNT(id) AS order_count FROM orders WHERE category = '电子产品' AND created_at >= '2024-01-01' AND created_at < '2025-01-01' GROUP BY DATE_FORMAT(created_at, '%Y-%m') ORDER BY sales_month LIMIT 500
返回 12 行数据
